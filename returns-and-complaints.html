Returns and Complaints

Withdrawal from contract

If you are a consumer, you may withdraw from the contract within 14 days from receipt of goods where legally applicable. To exercise this right, contact info@smirglpapir.com with your order number.

Return procedure

Returned goods must be sent back complete and without damage beyond normal handling. Return shipping costs are borne by the customer unless otherwise required by law.

Complaints procedure

For product defects, send a complaint to info@smirglpapir.com including order number, description of issue, and photos if possible. We will confirm receipt and provide next steps.

Complaint resolution

Complaints are handled without undue delay, no later than within statutory deadlines under Czech law. You will be informed by email about the result.
