Ferns

Earthy, subtle, and just a little wild – the Ferns are designed for boho homes with soul. Building on the minimalism of the Constances, this collection adds layers of green, brown, and warm natural tones. Think cut glass bottles reimagined with cement, trays dotted with green shards, and bowls lined with soft textiles. Every piece feels grounded yet welcoming, a quiet companion that brings warmth into everyday spaces.
