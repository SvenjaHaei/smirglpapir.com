Constances

Inspired by Japanese minimalism, the Constances bring clean lines and quiet sophistication into your home. Each piece is crafted from cement – from trays, bowls, and flower pots to vases paired with glass and playful wine stoppers blending cork and concrete. While the shapes are often simple and serene, some surprise with a wink of humor – a bowl shaped by the imprint of a cauliflower, or a wine stopper topped with a tiny dinosaur. Modern, refined, but never too serious.
